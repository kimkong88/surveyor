## Primary Personas

### Sarah Chen — Time-Pressed Homeowner (Primary)

-   Context: Organizing a household move while juggling work/family; contacted multiple movers.
-   Goals: Finish the survey quickly on mobile; avoid phone pressure; feel confident it’s “complete.”
-   Frictions: Aggressive sales calls, being asked for measurements, low bandwidth at times.
-   Devices: iPhone/Android (portrait), mobile browser.
-   Accessibility: Needs clear captions for voice guidance; large tap targets.
-   Success signal: Completes in one sitting (<30 minutes), no need for support, low edits post-session.

#### JTBD

When planning a move and requesting quotes, I want to capture an accurate inventory easily on my phone so I can get a fair estimate without scheduling visits or guessing measurements.

### Marcus Rodriguez — Quote-Pressed Moving Coordinator (Secondary)

-   Context: High volume of quote requests; needs speed without margin risk.
-   Goals: Trust the AI output, verify quickly, produce a quote in <10 minutes.
-   Frictions: Inaccurate phone estimates, time burned on site visits.
-   Devices: Desktop web; occasionally tablet.
-   Success signal: Opens PDF, verifies key items, generates confident quote in minutes.

#### JTBD

When a survey arrives, I need a concise, verifiable inventory with clear photos and CFT so I can price accurately in under 10 minutes.

## Experience Principles

-   Mobile-first, voice-guided, text-equivalent always available.
-   Confidence by design: Satisfactory Check blocks progress until completeness.
-   Low cognitive load: one clear task per screen with next best action.
-   Trust through transparency: show counts, caveats, and progress at all times.

## Key Scenarios

1. Sarah completes entire home with retakes prompted by Satisfactory Check; receives clear completion summary.
2. Marcus reviews room-organized PDF with numbered overlays; confirms high-impact items and quotes quickly.

## Success Metrics (mapped)

-   Homeowner completion rate ≥ 90%
-   Review-to-quote time < 10 minutes
-   Overall AI accuracy ≥ 96%, high-impact ≈ 99%
